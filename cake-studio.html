        GUIDE

        Tolerance, returns & replacements

        Print and cut alignment may vary by up to about 1.5 mm. That is why a 2 mm safe margin is shown between important artwork and the cut line.

        Eligible for remake or replacement

        • Print or cut misalignment clearly beyond the stated tolerance
        • A product or quantity different from the confirmed order
        • Print or cut defects caused by us
        • Significant damage during delivery
        • Food-quality defects, including abnormal baking colour

        Not eligible for return or replacement

        • Artwork approved in the finished preview
        • Alignment within the stated tolerance
        • Colour differences within the range explained before ordering
        • Changes requested for customer reasons
        • Deterioration caused by improper storage after delivery
