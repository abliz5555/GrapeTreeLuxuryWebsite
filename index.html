Budonomori Co. Ltd. — Japan

Founded in 1982 in Japan, Budonomori Co. Ltd. represents decades of refined craftsmanship in confectionery and luxury gifting. Rooted in Japanese tradition, the company focuses on precision, elegance, and attention to detail in every creation. Each product reflects a deep respect for heritage, combining artisanal techniques with timeless presentation. Over the years, Budonomori has become a symbol of quality and cultural authenticity in Japan’s premium dessert industry.

Grape Tree FZE — United Arab Emirates

Established in 2024, Grape Tree FZE brings Japanese craftsmanship into the UAE luxury market. We specialize in premium dessert artistry and luxury gifting experiences, blending traditional Japanese heritage with modern international branding. Our mission is to create elegant, high-end gifting solutions for customers across the Middle East and beyond. Today, Grape Tree FZE continues to grow as a bridge between Japan and the global luxury lifestyle industry.
